STRin

The digital guest guidebook for short-term rental hosts. Everything your guests need — check-in, codes, house rules and local favorites — in one link they open from their phone.

What STRin does

Guidebooks guests actually open

Check-in and check-out steps, room-by-room notes, photos and floor plans — shared by link or a printed QR card, no app download for guests.

Codes on a stay window

Door codes and Wi-Fi appear only during a guest's stay, synced from your booking calendars or set by hand. Outside the window, they stay hidden.

House rules & shared spaces

Bathroom and kitchen etiquette, laundry, parking and quiet hours — written once, shown to every guest the same way.

Local recommendations

Your favorite restaurants, sights and essentials, each with a map link and distance, grouped the way guests browse them.

Messaging & cleaner tools

Ready-made guest messages for every stage of the stay, plus a per-room turnover checklist your cleaner works from.

Feedback before reviews

Guests can report a problem or leave private feedback during the stay, so small issues get fixed before they become public reviews.

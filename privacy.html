Privacy Policy for STRin — Guest Guidebook

Last updated: November 21, 2026

STRin ("we", "our") is operated by Rock Lilly Pad LLC, 63 Rock Street, New Haven, CT 06511, USA. Contact: aly.iman@gmail.com. This policy explains what the STRin host app and guest guides collect, why, and the choices you have.

Who uses STRin

  • Hosts create an account and build digital guidebooks for their rental properties.
  • Guests open a guide through a link or QR code. Guests do not create accounts and do not download the app.

Information we collect

From hosts

  • Account details: name, email address, phone number (used in the guide's contact buttons and message features).
  • Property content the host enters: property names and addresses, room details, photos, check-in/check-out instructions, house rules, local recommendations, announcements, cleaner checklists, and message templates.
  • Access credentials for the property: door codes and Wi-Fi passwords the host enters so the guide can show them to that property's guests. These are sensitive: see "How codes are protected" below.
  • Calendar links: if a host connects a booking calendar (for example an Airbnb or Booking.com iCal export link) to automate stay windows, we store that link and the booking date ranges it contains. Calendar links are stored server-side only and are never displayed in the app after they are saved.
  • Payment information: subscriptions are processed by our payment processors. We do not store full card numbers.

From guests (no account)

  • Problem reports and private feedback the guest chooses to submit: the message, an optional rating, and the room/property it concerns.
  • Basic technical data needed to serve the guide (device/browser type, language preference) — used to display the guide in the guest's language.
  • We do not ask guests for names, emails, payment details, or location tracking.

Automatically

  • Standard server logs (IP address, timestamps, pages/actions requested) for security and reliability, retained for 90 days.

How codes are protected

Door codes and Wi-Fi passwords are shown to guests only within the visibility rules the host sets (for example, only during a booked stay window). Hosts can rotate a guide's link at any time, which invalidates previously shared links.

How we use information

  • To operate the guides: display host content to that host's guests, deliver reports and feedback to the host, sync calendars the host connected, and provide support.
  • To improve reliability and prevent abuse.
  • We do not sell personal information. We do not use guest data for advertising, and property content from one host is never shown to another host's guests.

Sharing

  • With service providers who run the service (hosting, email/message delivery, payments), only what they need to do their job.
  • If required by law, or to protect the safety of people or property.
  • A guest's report or feedback is shared with the host of that property — that is its purpose.

Retention and deletion

Hosts can edit or delete their property content at any time in the app, and can delete their account, which removes their guidebooks, codes, calendar links and reports. Guests may ask the host, or contact us at aly.iman@gmail.com, to delete a report or feedback they submitted.

Children

STRin is a tool for adult hosts and their adult guests; it is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect children's data.

Changes

We will post changes here and update the date above; material changes will be announced in the app before they take effect.
